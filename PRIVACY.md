# Privacy Policy for Music & Video Downloader Bot (@songsuperzxbot)

*Last Updated: November 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Music & Video Downloader Bot** (`@songsuperzxbot`, "we", "our", or "the Bot") handles, collects, stores, and protects your information when you interact with the service on Telegram.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency, minimal data collection, and zero permanent media storage.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide fast, reliable media downloading and manage fair server bandwidth, we collect only the minimal data necessary:

1. **Telegram Account Identifiers:**
   - **Telegram User ID:** A unique numerical ID provided by Telegram to identify your account.
   - **Username & First Name:** To personalize menus and referral links.
   - *Note: We never ask for or collect your phone number, password, or contact lists.*

2. **Search & Download Records:**
   - **Search Queries:** Song or artist names you search for, displayed in your personal `/history`.
   - **Download Metadata:** File title, format (MP3, M4A, MP4), selected resolution (e.g., 720p, 1080p), and timestamp. This data is used solely to calculate daily download quotas and VIP pass validity.

3. **Payment & Subscription Information:**
   - **UPI Reference / UTR Number (12 Digits):** Submitted manually by users to verify VIP plan activation.
   - **Plan Tier & Expiry Date:** Duration and daily allowance remaining on active passes.
   - *Important:* We **NEVER** collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, bank account credentials, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. Payments are conducted entirely within your own external UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

---

## 2. Zero Media Retention Guarantee

We operate a strict **no-permanent-media** policy:

- **Ephemeral Processing:** Any media file (video, reel, audio, thumbnail) processed by the bot is stored in an isolated, encrypted temporary folder solely for conversion and remuxing.
- **Immediate Disk Wipe:** The exact instant a file is delivered to your Telegram chat, the temporary file and working directory are **permanently wiped** from server storage.
- **No Hosting:** We do not host, archive, or re-distribute media files on our servers.

---

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect or Do

- ❌ We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties or advertisers.
- ❌ We **do not read private messages** in groups. The bot operates with Telegram Group Privacy Mode enabled.
- ❌ We **do not track your location or IP address**.
- ❌ We **do not spam** your Telegram inbox with unsolicited advertising.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The bot interacts with third-party APIs and content delivery networks solely upon your direct command:
- **Telegram:** Messages and files are transmitted via Telegram's MTProto encrypted infrastructure according to [Telegram's Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).
- **Media Sources (YouTube, Instagram, X/Twitter):** Public media information is retrieved on demand to fulfill user requests. Users are responsible for complying with the terms of service of the respective platforms.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security controls:
- **Least-Privilege Isolation:** The bot runs in a restricted, non-root Linux container environment.
- **SQL Injection Prevention:** All database operations utilize strict parameterized queries.
- **Brute-Force & Anti-Spam Protection:** Automated rate limiters and cooldown guards safeguard user data and server stability against unauthorized scraping or abuse.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:
- **View Your History:** You can inspect your recent downloads and searches anytime using the `/history` command.
- **Data Erasure:** You can request the permanent deletion of your account record, search logs, and download history at any time by contacting the developer at [@coder_chandan](https://t.me/coder_chandan). Requests are processed within 24 hours.
